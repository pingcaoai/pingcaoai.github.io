Misc / Thesis image design

My PhD thesis cover.

My research brings together AI-based embryo imaging and preimplantation genetic testing (PGT). The sequencing-read motif represents the genetic information analysed through PGT.

The central cross and four corner marks evoke a camera viewfinder, linking the composition to the image-based analysis at the core of my AI research.

The outer ring represents the trophectoderm—the cell layer sampled during embryo biopsy. The trophectoderm later gives rise to the trophoblast and contributes to the fetal part of the placenta, which interfaces with maternal uterine tissue. By contrast, the inner cell mass develops into the embryo proper and ultimately the fetus.

Variations in colour suggest the cellular diversity of a mosaic embryo. Because current biopsy methods sample the trophectoderm rather than the inner cell mass, the result may not fully represent the cells destined to form the fetus—an inherent limitation of biopsy-based embryo assessment.

Cover & embryo

12 earlier works
  1. 01

    Star-lit sphere

    The first study treats the embryo as a three-dimensional mesh. A single point of light gives the spherical structure depth and introduces the idea of a focal cell.

  2. 02

    Clean mesh sphere

    A denser mesh makes the embryo feel more spatial and biologically complex, moving it away from a flat diagram.

  3. 03

    Dissolving sphere

    The embryo begins to dissolve into particles, linking its physical structure to the image data used in computational analysis.

  4. 04

    Warm-focus embryo

    The warm point marks an internal area of interest, while the surrounding mesh retains the volume of the whole embryo.

  5. 05

    Gradient cover

    The three-dimensional embryo enters a cover composition for the first time, with the warm centre guiding the viewer's focus.

  6. 06

    Dark embryo cover

    The four corners and central cross work as a camera viewfinder, referring to the embryo imaging that began this research.

  7. 07

    Dark back-cover companion

    The short marks represent DNA sequencing reads, extending the story from embryo imaging to the genetic analysis that follows biopsy.

  8. 08

    Dark front cover

    This version brings the viewfinder, embryo and sequencing reads together, connecting image capture with genetic testing.

  9. 09

    Blue cover study

    The yellow cluster represents the inner cell mass. The blue outer ring represents the trophectoderm sampled during biopsy, and the lines to the right suggest sequencing reads.

  10. 10

    Da Vinci cover study

    The inner cell mass remains untouched while cells from the surrounding trophectoderm are used for sequencing. This means the biopsy may not fully represent the cells that will form the fetus.

  11. 11

    Zine cover study

    A smaller composition tests whether the relationship between embryo, biopsy and sequencing still reads clearly with more space around it.

  12. 12

    Ultramarine cover embryo

    The embryo is enlarged and opened outwards: the orange inner cell mass stays at the centre while blue sequencing reads extend from the trophectoderm.
